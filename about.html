About

Tin Can Hacks is where Brian Smith logs the tech and AI work done from a 1979 Airstream Sovereign: the projects that shipped, the ones that got abandoned honestly, and the ones that were only ever talked about at 60 mph. Most of it is built with Helix, an AI agent who writes the code, runs the tests, and occasionally writes the prose.

The archive is backfilled from the real work log, so dates match when the work actually happened rather than when the site went up.
